NoMoreReels Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

NoMoreReels is a small app that limits how long you spend in the apps you pick. We built it so your usage stays on your phone. Here's what it looks at, what leaves your phone (very little), and what you can do about it.

The accessibility service

NoMoreReels uses Android's Accessibility Service for one thing: knowing which app is open right now. It only sees the app's name (like com.example.app). That's how it counts your time and sends you back to the home screen when your time is up.

  • It can't see what's on your screen, your messages, your passwords or anything you type.
  • The app name is used on your phone and never sent, sold or shared.
  • We ask before you turn it on, and you can switch it off any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

What's saved on your phone

Your settings, the apps you chose to limit, and when you used them (start and end times, kept for about a week). This stays on your phone. The app doesn't back it up or send it anywhere, and uninstalling deletes it.

Ideas you send us (optional)

If you send an idea from the "Back this project" screen, the app sends us:

  • what you wrote,
  • the coffee amount and Google Play order number, if you bought one with it,
  • the app version and Android version.

It goes over an encrypted connection to a Google Form we own and is stored in our Google account (Google runs that service for us). We only use it to read your ideas and reply. We don't sell or share it. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Buying us a coffee (optional)

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play gives us the usual order info (order number, product and price), as explained in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Kids

NoMoreReels isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Your choices

You can stop everything by turning off the accessibility service or uninstalling the app. If you sent us an idea and want it deleted, email us with roughly when you sent it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
